the brand story

SO... WHY MEMES?

It started with a ridiculous little idea: what if the internet's funniest, most chaotic thoughts could live in your everyday life instead of just your browser tab?

One person was scrolling, laughing at the same stupid joke for the fourth time, and thinking, “I need this energy on a bag.” Then the next thought was, “Wait, why are all the cute things so serious?” And just like that, the whole thing clicked.

Memes are basically tiny pieces of joy that make a day better. They are the little spark in a boring meeting, the explanation for a weird mood, the universal language for “I feel seen.” So we turned that energy into totes and pins: useful things, yes, but also tiny capsules of personality.

We wanted to make products that feel like a wink from a friend. Not overly polished. Not trying too hard. Just funny, thoughtful, and a little chaotic in the best way.

We make things for people who want their stuff to feel like them.

founder

HI. I'M ELIZABETH.

Hi, I’m Elizabeth. I’m the kind of person who keeps screenshots of jokes because “this is going in my brand someday,” and somehow that turned into a whole business.

I started Whimsy because I wanted to make something that felt like the internet at its best: funny, honest, a little chaotic, and unexpectedly comforting. I love memes because they are a language for all the feelings we don’t always have elegant words for.

So I built a brand around the idea that your everyday objects shouldn’t be boring. Your tote can carry your groceries and your sense of humour. Your pin can say, “Yes, I have thoughts.”

I’m hoping to create a tiny corner of the world where people can carry a little personality everywhere they go. A place for the silly, the clever, the weird, the joyful, and the very much not serious.

— Elizabeth
